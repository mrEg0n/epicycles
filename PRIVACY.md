# Privacy

*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

Epicycles does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking, and the app contains no networking code: nothing you
do in it is sent anywhere.

## Photos and camera

The app asks for the camera only when you choose to take a photo. Photos picked
from the library come through the system picker, so the app sees just the photo
you select and has no access to the rest of your library.

Photos are processed on the device. Finding the subject of a photo uses Apple's
Vision framework, which also runs on the device.

## What is stored on your device

- Shapes you save in Draw: the silhouette and the cropped photo.
- Collections in Measure: the outlines, their descriptors, and the names and
  groups you type.
- Your display preferences.

All of this stays in the app's own storage and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Sharing

Data leaves the app only when you export it yourself — an equation, a spectrum, a
table of descriptors or a collection file — through the system share sheet, to
the destination you choose.
